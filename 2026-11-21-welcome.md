This website is a home for our research group at the Department of Agricultural Chemistry, Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University.

Students will find lecture notes, practical manuals and reading lists on the [Teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) page. Researchers can browse our [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and [international partners]({{ '/collaborations/' | relative_url }}).

I will also use this blog to write about food safety, pesticides, water and environmental chemistry in plain language. Many questions I hear from students, farmers and family members deserve clear, evidence-based answers: Is it safe to eat vegetables from the market? What does "residue" actually mean? How do we test fish for chemicals?

If there is a topic you would like me to write about, please [send me a message]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
